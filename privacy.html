Privacy Policy

Predictable Funding. Last updated October 6, 2026.

This policy explains what information Predictable Funding ("we", "us") collects when you fill out one of our forms, including forms in Facebook and Instagram ads, book a call with us, or reply to our messages, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a form or book a call, we collect the information you give us. That usually includes your name, email address, phone number, company name and website, your role, and your answers to qualifying questions such as company size and location. If you reach us through a Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead form, Meta passes us the details you submit in that form.

We also keep a record of our calls, emails and text messages with you, and of any meeting you book.

How we use it

  • To see whether your company qualifies for the opportunity you asked about.
  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about your inquiry, and to schedule, confirm and remind you of calls.
  • To prepare for and follow up on conversations with you.
  • To keep our records accurate and to improve our outreach.

Text messages and email

By submitting a form, you agree that we may contact you by text message and email about your inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text to stop receiving them, or reply HELP for help. You can unsubscribe from email at any time using the link in the email or by contacting us.

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information with service providers that help us run our business, such as our CRM, scheduling, email and text messaging providers. They may use it only to provide those services to us.

If your company is a fit and you agree to move forward, we may share your company and contact details with a partner organization as part of an introduction you have asked for. We will not do this without your agreement.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we have a business reason to, such as an open conversation or a legal or accounting requirement, and then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law (for example in Canada, the United Kingdom, or certain US states). To make a request, email us at the address below. We will respond within the time the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
